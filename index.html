How to Play & Set Up

Use your keyboard immediately, or turn on camera tracking.

① Follow the beat

Two lanes: LEFT ARM and RIGHT ARM. Watch the circular icons descend. Perform the shown movement when the icon crosses the glowing target line.

↑ UP↓ DOWN↔ SIDE⤾ TWIST & PUNCH

② Keyboard controls

Practice without a camera. Each key registers a move in its corresponding lane.

Left: 1 Up · 2 Side · 3 Twist · 4 Down

Right: Q Up · W Side · E Twist · R Down

③ Camera tracking (optional)

Allow camera access, stand back until both shoulders, elbows and wrists are visible, and keep the room bright. Pose tracking requires internet to load MediaPipe.

Camera off

④ Phone controller (experimental)

Phone motion assistance needs a pairing connection and motion permission. Keyboard and camera are available even without a phone.
