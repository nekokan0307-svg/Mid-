How to Play & Set Up

Use your keyboard immediately, or configure camera and phone in Settings.

Starting Position / ท่าเริ่มต้น

Stand facing the camera, arms relaxed by your sides, with both hands visible. Hold your phone securely in either hand.

UP: raise your hand overhead. SIDE: extend your arm sideways. DOWN: lower your arm. JUMPING JACK: raise both arms, then lower them. Jump if comfortable; arm-only movement earns the same score.

One music beat = one movement count. Keep both hands and shoulders visible.

① Follow the beat

Two lanes: LEFT ARM and RIGHT ARM. Watch the circular icons descend. Perform the shown movement when the icon crosses the glowing target line.

↑ UP↓ DOWN↔ SIDE👏 JUMPING JACK

② Keyboard controls

Practice without a camera. Each key registers a move in its corresponding lane.

Left: 1 Up · 2 Side · 3 Jack · 4 Down

Right: Q Up · W Side · E Jack · R Down

③ Camera & Phone

Camera and phone controls are configured in Settings. Use a well-lit space and keep both arms visible.
